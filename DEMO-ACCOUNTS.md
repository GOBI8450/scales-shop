# Scales demo: login accounts

Log in at the "כניסה" button, or open "רשימת 20 החנויות והסיסמאות" on the site and use "כניסה מהירה".
Store names are real Israeli reptile/pet businesses used only as templates. All descriptions, products, prices, sales numbers and orders are invented.
The "verified" mark (✔) is given in the demo to 10 bigger stores to show how the feature looks. It is illustrative only and says nothing about the real businesses' licences.
